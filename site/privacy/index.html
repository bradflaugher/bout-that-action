'Bout That Action

Privacy policy

Effective 2026-09-28 · Last updated 2026-10-12

'Bout That Action (package com.bradflaugher.aboutthataction), an Android game by Brad Flaugher, collects nothing.

What the app does not do

  • No network permission: the app cannot access the internet at all, so it can't send anything anywhere.
  • No accounts, analytics, ads, crash reports, or tracking of any kind.
  • No online leaderboards, and it never asks you for a rating.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (sound, difficulty, controls, text size, hero), your records (best score, deepest floor, runs played, challenges cleared and your best tries) and which tutorial tips you've already seen are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are not backed up to the cloud. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Links out

Share hands the game's Google Play link to your device's share sheet. Rate opens the game's page in the Play Store app, or in your browser. Feedback and Privacy policy open a web page in your browser. Those are other apps, and their own privacy policies apply from there; the game itself sends nothing.

Permissions

The only permission the app requests is vibration, used solely for haptics.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

This website

This site is static pages on GitHub Pages, with no cookies, analytics, or third-party scripts or fonts. GitHub serves it and may log technical details such as your IP address; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will change first, and the date above with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: brad@bradflaugher.com, or open an issue at github.com/bradflaugher/bout-that-action/issues.
