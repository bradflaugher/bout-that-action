# Privacy policy

'Bout That Action, a game by Brad Flaugher, collects nothing.

- **No data leaves your phone.** The app has no network permission, so it
  can't send anything anywhere: no accounts, analytics, ads, crash reports or
  tracking.
- **What stays on your phone:** your settings (sound, difficulty, controls,
  your hero) and your records (best score, deepest floor, runs played), in the
  app's private storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes
  them. They aren't backed up to the cloud.
- **Permissions:** vibration only, for haptics.
- **Links out:** SHARE hands the game's Play Store link to your phone's share
  sheet, and FEEDBACK and PRIVACY POLICY open a page in your browser. Those
  apps take it from there; the game itself still sends nothing.
- **Children:** the game collects nothing from anyone, children included.

If this ever changes, this page will change first.

Questions: open an issue at
<https://github.com/bradflaugher/bout-that-action/issues>.

*Effective 2026-09-28, last updated 2026-10-05.*
