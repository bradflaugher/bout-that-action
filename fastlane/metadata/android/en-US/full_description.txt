A helicopter drops you on the roof of a skyscraper. Then you go down, and down, and down.

'Bout That Action is an endless, portrait-only action game: a neon-noir take on the arcade classic Elevator Action, with a cardboard box to hide in. Ride elevators down through the Neon Tower, the Black Labs, the Deep Metro, the Iron Mines and the Magma Core, into Hell, and past it into a Void where anything goes.

BUILT FOR THUMBS
• Drag to run, swipe up to jump, swipe down to hide in a doorway or pop the box.
• Tap to ride an elevator, go through a passage or raid a STASH.
• Walk into a guard for a silent takedown. Land on heads to stomp.

TWO WAYS TO PLAY
• GUNS HOT: you auto-fire at anything in range.
• SILENT: you never fire, guards only notice what they see, and quiet kills pay double.

FOUR HEROES
A running back in full pads, a gentleman spy, a barefoot cop having the worst night ever, and a jungle commando who lives in the box. Each has their own trait, three perks of their own and their own take on the music.

EVERY FLOOR IS NEW
The building never ends and never repeats. Floors, the soundtrack and every sound effect are generated as you play. Blackouts, nap time and payday floors keep things weird.

YOUR GAME, YOUR RULES
Pick a difficulty from CHILL to STRAIGHT TO HELL, or tune the heat curve yourself. Share a seed to play the same building as a friend.

NO NONSENSE
No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts. No network. No daily rewards or timers nagging you to come back. The only permission is vibration.
