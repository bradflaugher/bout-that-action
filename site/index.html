How it plays

Every floor is a little puzzle

Two to four hallways joined by passage doors, the whole hallway always on screen, and the ride down is always somewhere else. The choice is how: which door, which hallway's guards, which lift, and when to move.

Guns hot or silent

GUNS HOT fires itself at whoever's about to hurt you. SILENT never fires: take guards from behind, wait them out, black out the lights. Quiet kills pay double.

The box is a lure

Move it while a guard is watching and he comes over to check. Let him. Heavies and ninjas aren't fooled: they kick it off.

Elevators only

No stairs. Short hops, or a rare gold express that drops three to five floors at once and opens its doors on the way, onto someone waiting.

Special floors

BLACKOUT, every light dead, for them too. NAP TIME, tiptoe up for a NIGHT NIGHT. PAYDAY, somebody's bonus lying around. Some rides come with smooth jazz.

Roguelike runs

Every gold STASH offers three perks, and they stack: Ricochet, Ghost Box, Double Jump, Shockwave bonks and more. Enemies drop shotguns, miniguns and slow-mo.

Endless and seeded

Any floor rebuilds from a seed, so the building never ends. Every run has a code like K7QM 2XAB: send it, and your friend plays your exact building.

Silly names, real goals

Reach a floor, ghost three floors, bonk a dozen guards, with a twist or two: SILENT ONLY, ONE HEART, UNTOUCHED. Five tiers, ROOKIE to LEGEND. Any run that meets one clears it, so there's nothing to grind, and a daily pick everyone shares.

Synthesized live

Every note and every bang is generated as you play: a procedural track per zone, a sneak mix for SILENT that swaps in on the next beat, a title theme called "Going Down", and every hero bringing their own band. Getting spotted gets a sting; the music goes to ALERT.
