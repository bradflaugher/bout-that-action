A helicopter drops you on the roof of a skyscraper. Then you go down, and down, and down.

'Bout That Action is an endless, portrait-only action game: a neon-noir spy caper in the spirit of the 80s arcade, with a cardboard box to hide in. Ride elevators down through the Neon Tower, the Black Labs, the Deep Metro, the Iron Mines and the Magma Core, into Hell, and past it into a Void where anything goes.

BUILT FOR THUMBS
• Drag to run, swipe up to jump, swipe down to hide in a doorway or pop the box.
• Tap to ride an elevator, go through a passage or raid a STASH (if nobody's hunting you).
• Sneak up behind a guard for a silent takedown. Land on his head to daze him first. (The monkey can't: he brought a big gun instead.)

TWO WAYS TO PLAY
• GUNS HOT: you auto-fire at anything in range.
• SILENT: you never fire, guards only notice what they see, and quiet kills pay double.
• Except the monkey. He's always guns hot. He spray-painted the tutorial billboard to make sure you know.

FOUR HEROES
Bull the bruiser, Fox the high-kicking street brawler, Hawk the parcel courier who lives in a box and Monkey the circus runaway with a very big gun. Each has their own trait, three perks of their own and their own take on the music.

EVERY FLOOR IS NEW
The building never ends and never repeats. Floors, the soundtrack and every sound effect are generated as you play. Blackouts, nap time and payday floors keep things weird.

YOUR GAME, YOUR RULES
Pick a difficulty from CHILL to STRAIGHT TO HELL, or tune the heat curve yourself. Every run has a short seed code: share it, and a friend plays the very same building.

NO NONSENSE
Phones, tablets and foldables: it always plays in portrait, and pauses itself when a call, the notification shade or unplugged headphones interrupt.

No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts. No network. No daily rewards or timers nagging you to come back. The only permission is vibration.
